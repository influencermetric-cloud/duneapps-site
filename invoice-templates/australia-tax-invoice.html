Invoices

Australian tax invoice template, with the ATO's seven details

The ATO's rule is short enough to fit on a card: seven details under $1,000, an eighth from $1,000. The template carries all eight, so it works at any amount.

By Nahid SaleemUpdated 15 November 20261 min read

Checked against ATO: Tax invoices on 15 November 2026.

Key takeaways
  • Under $1,000 a tax invoice must show seven things: that it is a tax invoice, your identity, your ABN, the date, what you sold with quantity and price, the GST, and how far each item is taxable.
  • From $1,000, add the buyer's identity or ABN.
  • If a customer asks, you must provide a tax invoice within 28 days, unless the sale is $82.50 or less including GST.
  • The GST can be shown as an amount, or, if it is exactly one eleventh of the total, as the words "Total price includes GST".

The ATO's own shortcut: if your tax invoices meet the requirements for sales of $1,000 or more, you can use them for smaller sales too. That is why this template carries the buyer's details on every version.

When you have to provide one

If a customer asks for a tax invoice, you must give them one within 28 days, unless the sale was $82.50 or less including GST. Below that amount a business customer can claim the GST credit from a receipt or a docket. Above it, they need a tax invoice from you, so most business customers will ask.

Questions people ask

What must an Australian tax invoice show?

For a taxable sale of less than $1,000, the ATO lists seven details: that the document is intended to be a tax invoice, the seller's identity, the seller's ABN, the date of issue, a brief description of what was sold with the quantity and price, the GST amount payable, and the extent to which each sale on the invoice is taxable. For sales of $1,000 or more, add the buyer's identity or ABN.

Do I have to give a tax invoice for a small sale?

Only if the customer asks, and then within 28 days, unless the sale is $82.50 or less including GST. A business customer needs a tax invoice to claim a GST credit on a purchase above $82.50.

Can I write 'Total price includes GST' instead of the GST amount?

Yes, where the GST is exactly one eleventh of the total price. Otherwise show the GST amount.

How long do I keep tax invoices in Australia?

Five years is the ATO's general rule for records.
