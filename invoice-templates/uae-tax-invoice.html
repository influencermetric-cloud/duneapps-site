Invoices

UAE tax invoice template, with every field the FTA requires

A UAE tax invoice is not a format choice. Article 59 of the VAT Executive Regulation lists what it must show, and an invoice in dollars that leaves the VAT out of dirhams is not a valid tax invoice at all.

By Nahid SaleemUpdated 15 November 20263 min read

Checked against FTA Public Clarification VATP006 on tax invoices and Ministerial Decision 244 of 2025 on 15 November 2026.

Key takeaways
  • Heading "Tax Invoice", plus your name, address and TRN. Your customer's name, address and TRN too when they are registered.
  • On a full tax invoice every line shows the unit price, quantity, VAT rate and amount in AED, and the invoice shows the gross total in AED.
  • Invoicing in dollars or euros is allowed, but the VAT must also be shown in AED, with the exchange rate used.
  • A simplified tax invoice is enough when the customer is not registered, or is registered and the supply is AED 10,000 or less.
  • From 1 July 2027, businesses under AED 50 million revenue must issue B2B invoices through e-invoicing. Sales to consumers stay outside it.

Full or simplified

Article 59(5) allows a simplified tax invoice in two cases: the customer is not registered for VAT, or the customer is registered and the supply is AED 10,000 or less. A simplified invoice can show its lines at the gross value with the VAT on a separate line at the bottom. A full one shows each line net, with its own VAT. Either way it has to be issued and delivered. The FTA's clarification is blunt about shops that only print one when asked: "This is not optional."

Invoicing in another currency

You can invoice a customer in dollars. The FTA says the invoice must then show "the tax amount payable expressed in AED" and "the exchange rate applied", at the UAE Central Bank's published rate on the date of supply, and that invoices which do not are "failing to issue a valid tax invoice for VAT purposes." The fill-in version below asks for the rate as soon as you pick a currency other than AED.

Rounding is per line: where the VAT on a line comes to a fraction of a fils, round it to the nearest fils on that line.

#What e-invoicing changes in 2027

The UAE is moving business invoices onto an electronic system run through accredited service providers. Ministerial Decision 244 of 2025 sets the dates, and the Ministry of Finance's guidelines of 26 October 2026 confirm them:

WhoAppoint a service provider byInvoice through the system by
Revenue of AED 50 million or more31 October 20261 January 2027
Revenue under AED 50 million31 March 20271 July 2027
Government entities31 March 20271 October 2027

Two things matter for a small business. Sales to consumers are outside it: the decision says business-to-consumer transactions "shall not be subject to the Electronic Invoicing System". Sales to other businesses and to government are inside it, so from 1 July 2027 a PDF invoice to a business customer, from this template or from any app that makes PDFs, is no longer the invoice that counts. Until then, a tax invoice with every field above is what the law asks for.

Keep every tax invoice you issue for five years, and fifteen for anything to do with real estate. What UAE VAT requires you to keep covers the rest of the records.

Questions people ask

What must a UAE tax invoice show?

On a full tax invoice: the words Tax Invoice; your name, address and TRN; your customer's name, address and TRN where they are registered; a sequential or unique invoice number; the date of issue and the date of supply if different; a description; for each line the unit price, quantity, VAT rate and amount payable in AED; any discount; the gross total in AED; and the VAT in AED.

When can I issue a simplified tax invoice in the UAE?

Article 59(5) allows one when the customer is not registered for VAT, or when the customer is registered and the supply is AED 10,000 or less. It still needs the words Tax Invoice, your name, address and TRN, the date of issue, a description, and the total with the VAT amount. You still have to issue it: the FTA says offering to print one on request does not meet the obligation.

Can I invoice in US dollars in the UAE?

Yes, but the FTA's clarification says the invoice must show the VAT payable in AED and the exchange rate applied, at the UAE Central Bank's rate on the date of supply. An invoice in a foreign currency without the VAT in dirhams is not a valid tax invoice.

Will a PDF invoice still work after UAE e-invoicing starts?

For sales to consumers, yes: business-to-consumer transactions are outside the e-invoicing system under Ministerial Decision 244 of 2025. For sales to businesses and government, no. A business with revenue under AED 50 million must appoint an accredited service provider by 31 March 2027 and invoice through the system by 1 July 2027; above AED 50 million it applied from 1 January 2027.
