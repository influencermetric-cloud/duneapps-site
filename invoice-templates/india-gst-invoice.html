GST invoice template for India, with every Rule 46 field

Rule 46 of the CGST Rules lists the particulars a tax invoice must carry, from a serial number of at most sixteen characters to the signature at the bottom. Most free templates leave three or four of them out.

By Nahid SaleemUpdated 15 November 20263 min read

Checked against CGST Rule 46, the CBIC note on HSN digits and the GST e-invoice portal on 15 November 2026.

Key takeaways
  • Supplier name, address and GSTIN; a serial number of up to 16 characters, unique for the financial year; the date.
  • Customer name, address and GSTIN if registered. An unregistered customer at ₹50,000 or more also needs the delivery address and state name and code.
  • HSN or SAC: 4 digits on B2B invoices up to ₹5 crore turnover, 6 digits above it.
  • Same state: CGST + SGST, half each. Different state: IGST, with the place of supply and its state.
  • Reverse charge Yes or No, and a signature or digital signature. Above ₹5 crore turnover, B2B invoices need an IRN from the e-invoice portal.

HSN digits

Turnover in the previous financial yearHSN or SAC on the invoice
Up to ₹5 crore4 digits on B2B invoices; may be left off invoices to unregistered buyers
More than ₹5 crore6 digits on every invoice for taxable supplies

The CBIC's own summary: "A taxpayer having turnover of upto five crore in the preceding financial year is required to mandatorily furnish 4 digits HSN code on B2B invoices." The 6-digit codes are the same ones used in the customs tariff.

CGST and SGST, or IGST

When you and the place of supply are in the same state, the GST rate is charged as two equal halves: 18% becomes CGST 9% and SGST 9%. When they are in different states, the whole rate is IGST, and the invoice must name the place of supply and its state. Untick "same state" on the fill-in version and it switches to IGST.

#When this template is not enough

Two kinds of business need something else. Above ₹5 crore aggregate turnover, B2B invoices have to be registered on the Invoice Registration Portal, which returns an IRN and a QR code to print on the invoice; the GST portal says a B2B invoice without a valid IRN is not valid. A paper or PDF template cannot do that step for you. And a business that is not registered for GST does not issue a tax invoice at all, which is why the fill-in version drops the GST fields when you untick "I am GST registered".

Questions people ask

What must a GST tax invoice contain?

Rule 46 lists it: supplier name, address and GSTIN; a consecutive serial number of up to sixteen characters, unique for the financial year; the date; the customer's name, address and GSTIN if registered; the HSN or SAC code; a description; quantity and unit for goods; total value; taxable value after any discount; the rate and amount of CGST, SGST or IGST; the place of supply with the state name for inter-state supplies; the delivery address if different; whether tax is payable on reverse charge; and a signature or digital signature.

How many HSN digits do I need on a GST invoice?

Based on turnover in the previous financial year. Up to ₹5 crore, 4 digits are mandatory on B2B invoices, and B2C invoices to unregistered buyers can leave HSN out. Above ₹5 crore, 6 digits on every taxable invoice. This has applied since 1 April 2021 under Notification 78/2020.

Is a signature required on a GST invoice?

Rule 46 includes a signature or digital signature of the supplier or an authorised representative. A proviso removes it for an electronic invoice issued in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000. Whether an emailed PDF counts as that is worth confirming with your CA; signing the invoice removes the question.

When do I need an e-invoice instead?

When your aggregate turnover is above ₹5 crore in any financial year from 2017-18. From 1 August 2023, those businesses must register B2B invoices on the Invoice Registration Portal and print the IRN and QR code. A template like this one is not an e-invoice. B2C invoices do not need an IRN.
