Why it matters

Claim what you are owed,
without handing over your life

A business mile is worth 76 cents. A year of receipts is worth thousands. Both are easy to lose track of — and most apps that help you keep them are quietly keeping a copy for themselves.

76¢

What every business mile is worth on a US return from 1 July 2026 (72.5¢ before). A thousand miles is $760 you can claim.

10 sec

A week is all the upkeep. Swipe each drive left or right and you are done.

3 yrs

How long the IRS expects you to be able to produce the record. Five for the UK self-employed and the UAE, six in Canada.

Free

Tracking and scanning never stop and never cost anything. No monthly cap to hit mid-month.
